Questions

The things people ask before they email.

How long does it take?
A one-page site is usually ready in three to five days. A larger site takes one to two weeks. The slowest part is almost always waiting on photos and menu text, so the sooner you send those, the sooner you're live.
What do I need to give you?
Your menu or product list, opening hours, a phone number and address, and any photos you already have. If you don't have good photos, tell me — I'll show you what to shoot on a phone, or we sort out a photographer.
Who owns the site and the domain?
You do, completely. The domain is registered in your name and the site is yours to keep. If you ever want to move to someone else, you take everything with you — no lock-in, no hostage situation.
What happens when my menu or prices change?
Send me the change and it's live the same day. Small updates are included for the first three months on the Complete package; after that they're a small fixed fee, or you can take a care plan and stop thinking about it.
Can I edit it myself?
If you want to, yes — I can set the site up so you can change text, prices and photos yourself, and I'll walk you through it. Most owners would rather just message me, which is why updates are part of what I offer.
Will people actually find it on Google?
Every site is built to load fast and be read properly by Google, and I set up your Google Business listing so you appear on Maps. That's what gets a hungry tourist standing 200 metres away to walk through your door.
Do you work with businesses outside Croatia?
Yes. I've built for a business in Vienna and work remotely without any trouble. Everything happens over email, phone or video, in Croatian, English, German or Italian.
